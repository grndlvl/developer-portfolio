## Recommended First Outreach Wave

1. Augusta Ball Hockey League
2. Augusta Boxing Club
3. Augusta United Dawgs
4. Bannja Shop
5. Top Dog Comics

Augusta Ball Hockey League is probably the strongest complement to the existing grndlvl Launch portfolio. It extends the existing club and sports proof while providing a clear before-and-after story around discovery, communication, and joining.

Bannja Shop is the strongest option for adding a visually different product-business example.

## Qualification Needed Before Outreach

Before contacting a candidate, verify:

- Its current Google Business Profile and linked website
- Recent social activity and signs that the organization is still operating
- The appropriate owner or decision-maker
- Public contact information
- Whether an unindexed replacement website already exists
- The most important customer action currently causing friction

This is a prospect shortlist, not yet a fully qualified lead list.
