## Important Fit Constraint

Beanie Ben's does not fit a strict “new business with no site” campaign because it already owns and operates `beaniebens.com`.

The prospect should only be pursued if research identifies a concrete improvement with commercial value, such as:

- Customers cannot easily view the current menu or prices
- Hours, specials, or ordering information are inconsistent across channels
- Catering and private-event inquiries lack a clear conversion path
- The family and San Antonio brand story is not being used effectively
- Local search and structured restaurant information are incomplete
- The current site creates measurable accessibility, mobile, performance, or usability friction
- The business needs an owned customer channel beyond third-party ordering and social platforms

If no meaningful gap exists, recommend disqualification rather than manufacturing a problem.

## Qualification Tasks

### 1. Confirm the Business Is Active

Verify:

- Current operating status
- Address, phone number, and hours
- Owner or primary decision-maker names
- Current menu and pricing
- Current ordering options
- Catering and private-event availability
- Whether the owners still operate the business directly

Prefer the business website, official social accounts, current menus, chamber records, and recent reporting.

### 2. Inventory the Current Digital Presence

Find and record:

- Official website and all public pages
- Official Facebook page
- Official Instagram account
- Google Business Profile
- Apple Maps and Bing Places listings, if present
- Online-ordering provider
- Delivery-platform listings
- Current menu destinations
- Review profiles
- Chamber and tourism listings

Check for inconsistent addresses, suite numbers, phone numbers, hours, menu prices, and links.

### 3. Evaluate the Existing Website

Review `beaniebens.com` on mobile and desktop. Determine whether a customer can quickly:

- Understand what Beanie Ben's serves
- View a complete, current menu with prices
- Confirm today's hours
- Find the restaurant
- Order ahead
- Understand dine-in, takeout, and delivery options
- Request catering or a private event
- Learn about the owners and the San Antonio inspiration
- See dietary or allergen information, if published
- Contact the restaurant without using a third-party ordering service

Record evidence, not aesthetic opinions. Avoid claims such as “the site is too simple” unless that simplicity causes a specific customer or business problem.

### 4. Identify Evidence of Ability to Pay

Use non-invasive public indicators such as:

- A staffed physical restaurant
- Recent hiring activity
- Current review count and recency
- Online ordering and delivery operations
- Catering or private-event offerings
- Chamber membership and ribbon-cutting participation
- Investment in a downtown storefront, equipment, signage, and branding
- Consistent activity across published business channels

Do not estimate revenue, profitability, credit, or the owners' personal finances.

### 5. Identify the Best Commercial Opportunity

Determine which, if any, of these opportunities has the strongest evidence:

1. Catering and private-event lead generation
2. Better owned menu and ordering experience
3. Local SEO and accurate restaurant structured data
4. Email or SMS capture for specials and weekend menus
5. First-party customer relationship independent of delivery platforms
6. Accessibility, performance, or mobile improvements
7. Stronger brand storytelling and press/review proof

Recommend only the smallest scope that solves a demonstrated problem.

## Outreach Positioning

Do not criticize the current website or imply that the owners have no online presence. Recognize that they launched quickly and already provide online ordering.

Lead with a verified growth opportunity. For example:

> Beanie Ben's already has a memorable concept, a strong family story, and an easy-to-understand reason to visit. I noticed an opportunity to make the owned web experience work harder for catering, current menu discovery, and local search while keeping ordering simple.

Only use that positioning if research confirms those gaps.

Connect the proposal to outcomes such as:

- Converting catering and private-event interest
- Reducing repeated questions about hours, menu items, and specials
- Keeping customers on an owned brand experience before sending them to order
- Helping search engines and AI assistants describe the restaurant accurately
- Building a direct audience for weekend specials and future offers
